OnBase connects builders, contractors and site managers with skilled workers on demand — brick masons, tilers, plasterers and helpers — for fast, reliable dispatch.

Book by evening and your worker is confirmed within the hour, then dispatched to arrive at your gate before the site opens the next morning. Same-day requests are also matched where a worker is free.

Every worker on the roster is ID-checked and screened for trade experience before they're allowed to accept bookings. You'll see their trade, experience, and rate up front — no surprises on site.

Pricing is a fixed daily rate per worker, shown before you book, with zero platform booking fees. No hidden charges, no hourly surprises, and you're charged only once the day's work is done.

Workers come with their own basic tools for the trade. For specialized equipment or materials specific to your site, you'll need to provide them.
